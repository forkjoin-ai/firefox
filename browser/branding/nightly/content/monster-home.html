Monster Studio

Physics, mass, and handoff-consensus passes composited into one animated 3D mass. Monster Studio loads .ply point-cloud and mesh layers, stacks them as live consensus passes, and brings the whole thing to life on WebGPU. Built on aeon-3d and Edgework.

How the composite works

  • Consensus passes. Physics, mass, and handoff scenes are separate .ply layers, composited and reconciled into a single animated form.
  • Point clouds + meshes. The viewer streams large point-cloud and mesh assets and renders them with the aeon-3d sovereign engine.
  • WebGPU first. The studio prefers WebGPU and falls back to WebGL, with a presentation mode and an overview camera for walkthroughs.
